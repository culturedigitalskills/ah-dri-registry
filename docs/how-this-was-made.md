# How this registry was made

**Purpose.** 
This registry was initiated as part of the community activities within the [Connecting Arts and Humanities for AI Capability] (UKRI7748), funded by UKRI. It was created after identifying that a common barrier researchers face in using artificial intelligence infrastructure is not knowing what is available, where to access it, how much it costs, or how the technologies' specifications might affect the research they want to perform. The idea of creating a community-owned registry of UK digital research infrastructure, compiled from public sources, was born.

This registry is the result of this initial effort. It is not a final output but a work in progress, available to researchers and practitioners to use, refer to, correct, extend, and own.

The registry is designed to include UK institutional, regional, national, European and international resources available to UK researchers. It also includes public and private compute, tools, and AI services.

**Who compiled it.** The registry was initially compiled by the project lead (Karina Rodriguez Echavarria, University of Brighton) in November 2026. The scope, structure, and wording were decided after reviewing other infrastructure registries, and she is responsible for the content of this first version. Later contributors are credited in each entry's "Collected by" field and in the repository history.

**Use of AI.** As a project about AI capability, we used AI ourselves, and want to be open about how. The first version was drafted with the help of Claude (Anthropic; model Claude Opus 5.5). Claude searched public web pages, suggested the field structure (based on the EOSC Service Profile v5.0), drafted entries for Karina to review, and wrote the scripts that build the API.

**Sources.** Only public web pages: providers' own websites, documentation, terms of use and funder guidance. Many entries cite the provider's page in their Notes. No personal data or unpublished material was entered into the AI tool. Contacts are team addresses, not individuals.

**What needs checking.** If any information is not clear, contributors can add `[to be confirmed]`  to indicate the information has not been verified. "Last checked" shows when each entry was last reviewed, and the [report](https://culturedigitalskills.github.io/ah-dri-registry/api/v1/report.json) lists entries with missing fields or open questions.

**Copyright.** The registry records facts and links; descriptions are written in our own words, not copied from providers. We recognise many concerns about AI models. Using an AI tool for this registry has allowed us to make a first draft available for others to benefit from without having to search the same information individually. We disclose it so readers can judge for themselves.

**Conflict of interest.** Claude is made by Anthropic, which also appears in the AI services collection. Anthropic's entries were filled against the same criteria as every other provider. Please flag anything that looks unfair.

**Environmental cost.** Using AI has an energy and carbon cost. The registry asks compute providers to publish energy information, and we would welcome the same transparency from AI providers.
