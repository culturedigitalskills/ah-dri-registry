# How this registry was made

**Purpose.** A starting draft of the compute, tools and AI services available to UK arts and humanities researchers. It is meant for the community to correct, extend and own.

**Who compiled it.** Compiled by Karina (University of Brighton) for the DRI project, November 2026. Karina decided the scope, structure and wording, and is responsible for the content of this first version. Later contributors are credited in each entry's "Collected by" field and in the repository history.

**Use of AI.** The first version was drafted with the help of Claude (Anthropic; model Claude Opus 5.5). Claude searched public web pages, suggested the field structure (based on the EOSC Service Profile v5.0), drafted entries for Karina to review, and wrote the scripts that build the API.

**Sources.** Only public web pages: providers' own websites, documentation, terms of use and funder guidance. Many entries cite the provider's page in their Notes. No personal data or unpublished material was entered into the AI tool. Contacts are team addresses, not individuals.

**What has been checked.** Fields marked `[to be confirmed]` have not been verified. Other entries come from cited sources but have not yet all been checked by a person. "Last checked" shows when each entry was last reviewed, and the [report](https://culturedigitalskills.github.io/ah-dri-registry/api/v1/report.json) lists entries with missing fields or open questions.

**Copyright.** The registry records facts and links; descriptions are written in our own words, not copied from providers. We recognise the concerns of many in the arts and humanities about AI models, including Claude, being trained on creative and scholarly work without permission; in 2025 Anthropic agreed to settle a US class action brought by authors over this. Using an AI tool here is not an endorsement of those practices. We disclose it so readers can judge for themselves.

**Conflict of interest.** Claude is made by Anthropic, which also appears in the AI services collection. Anthropic's entries were filled against the same criteria as every other provider. Please flag anything that looks unfair.

**Environmental cost.** Using AI has an energy and carbon cost. The registry asks compute providers to publish energy information, and we would welcome the same transparency from AI providers.
